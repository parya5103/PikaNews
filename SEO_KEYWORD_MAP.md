## Research notes

- The phrase clusters were selected from the site's actual categories, current article inventory, and a review of current English-language news search-result pages.
- Google Search Console's available domain report (2026-10-19 to 2026-11-15) showed only 5 impressions for the PikaNews root page in the India filter and no clicks. That sample is too small to infer reliable query demand or ranking trends.
- Numeric search volume, CPC and keyword difficulty are intentionally not invented. The connected keyword-research account had only 5 credits remaining; a full DataForSEO batch would exceed that balance.
- The terms are implemented in concise titles, descriptions, visible category introductions and internal category links. Meta-keywords tags are not used as a ranking tactic.
- PikaNews is an aggregator of short summaries with publisher attribution and source links. Avoid claims such as “live news”, “breaking news 24/7”, “exclusive reporting” or “verified facts” unless the product and editorial process actually support them.
- Do not target Hindi-language queries until the site publishes and maintains Hindi-language content.

## Measurement

After deployment, use Search Console query and page reports to replace assumptions with actual impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. Reassess clusters after enough data accumulates; do not add keyword variants merely to repeat phrases.
