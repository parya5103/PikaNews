## Editorial and measurement guardrails

- Match keywords to the actual language, category inventory and article content; do not target Hindi phrases until maintained Hindi content exists.
- PikaNews is a news aggregator with short summaries, publisher attribution and source links. Do not claim exclusive reporting, fact verification or 24/7 breaking coverage.
- Do not invent search volume, CPC or difficulty. Use Search Console query/page data after deployment to review impressions, clicks, CTR and average position.
- Third-party article pages remain noindex; only fresh, source-diverse topic hubs that pass the collector's quality gates are eligible for the regular sitemap. Canonical static article pages are indexable only for explicitly reviewed original PikaNews editorial.
