General Enquiries

For questions, feedback, partnership or advertising enquiries, email officialgogocompany@gmail.com. Please mention PikaNews in the subject line.

Correction, Attribution, Copyright, Privacy and Removal Requests

If a PikaNews article needs a correction, better attribution, or removal (copyright, privacy or other legal reasons), fill the form below. It opens your own email app with everything pre-filled, addressed to officialgogocompany@gmail.com. Nothing is sent silently: you press send in your own mail app, so nothing can be submitted without your knowledge.

We cannot act on anonymous requests that give no way to reply. Every request is tracked with Pending, Under Review, Resolved or Rejected status; see the Editorial & Corrections Policy for how requests are handled. For disputes, the affected article can be unpublished from PikaNews while the review is pending.

Your email app should open with the request pre-filled. If it does not, email officialgogocompany@gmail.com directly with the same details.

Response Times

We aim to acknowledge requests within a few days. Takedown requests for copyright or privacy are treated as urgent and can also be sent directly to officialgogocompany@gmail.com with the PikaNews article URL and the original source URL.
