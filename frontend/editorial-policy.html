Editorial & Corrections Policy

PikaNews is a digital platform by GoGo Company. This policy explains our editorial standards and correction procedures. Last updated: November 2026.

1. What PikaNews Is

PikaNews is an automated news discovery and aggregation platform. It collects headlines, short excerpts and links from the public RSS feeds of third-party publishers. It is not a newsroom and does not employ reporters. We do not pretend that aggregated excerpts are original reporting: every article page names the original publisher, links to the original report, and is labelled as a third-party source.

2. What We Never Do

  • We do not rewrite publishers' headlines into misleading clickbait.
  • We do not fabricate facts, quotes, numbers, images or context.
  • We do not publish full copies of copyrighted articles or use unlicensed photographs.
  • We do not invent author identities or bylines. Author names appear only when the source's own feed provides them.
  • We do not mass-produce thin pages to chase search keywords.
  • We do not show view counts, share counts or subscriber numbers that we do not really measure.

3. Content Quality Standards

Our pipeline automatically checks every collected story:

  • Duplicate detection: stories with the same normalized title and URL are collected once.
  • Thin-content detection: entries with extremely short or empty headlines or excerpts are not promoted to the homepage lead position, are not given their own article page, are not included in the Google News sitemap, and are not placed next to advertising.
  • Malformed entries (missing URL, missing title, invalid dates) are dropped during collection.
  • Feed failures never erase previously collected stories.

4. Corrections

Because excerpts come directly from publishers, most errors originate at the source and are usually fixed when the publisher updates its own report. When we are told about a problem:

  • Submit it via our content request page with the PikaNews URL and, if known, the original source URL.
  • We acknowledge every request and review it against the original publisher's report.
  • If our excerpt or attribution is wrong, we correct or remove the entry and regenerate the affected pages, sitemaps and feeds.
  • If the problem is in the publisher's own report, we remove the entry from PikaNews on request and point readers to the publisher.

5. Takedowns and Removal

Copyright, privacy, defamation and other removal requests are handled through the same content request page or by email to officialgogocompany@gmail.com.

  • Requests are tracked with Pending, Under Review, Resolved and Rejected statuses.
  • Disputed content can be unpublished from PikaNews while a review is pending.
  • Unpublished or deleted stories are added to a permanent blocklist so they cannot be re-imported from the feed later, and their generated pages, sitemap entries and feed entries are removed.
  • We retain a minimal audit record of removals (article identifier, source URL, action, reason, timestamp) for accountability. We cannot delete content from the original publisher's website or from search-engine caches; those must be requested from the respective parties.

6. Advertising Standards

Advertising is always labelled "Advertisement" and is never styled as a news story or placed inside navigation. We do not allow ads next to thin, empty or under-construction content, forced redirects, popups, or layouts designed for accidental clicks. Sponsored content, if ever introduced, would be clearly labelled "Sponsored" and kept separate from aggregated news.

7. Automation and Human Oversight

Collection, categorization and page generation are automated. Category assignment is keyword-based and can misclassify a story; if you spot a misclassified story, tell us via the contact page and we will adjust the pipeline. A human reviews all corrections, takedown and quality complaints.
