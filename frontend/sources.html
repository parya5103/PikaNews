How Sources Are Selected

PikaNews reads the public RSS feeds of established publishers. Selection criteria:

  • The publisher offers a public, working RSS feed.
  • The publisher is a recognized outlet for its category.
  • The feed provides a headline, a short excerpt and a link to the full report on the publisher's own site.

Feeds that fail for an extended period are tracked in our pipeline and reviewed; existing stories are never erased because one feed fails. We do not accept payment to include a source in this list, and sponsored content, if ever introduced, would be clearly labelled and kept out of the news flow.

What We Publish From Each Story

  • The headline as published by the source.
  • A short excerpt or summary, as provided by the source's own feed.
  • The publisher's name and a link to the original report.
  • When the source's feed provides one, the source's own image for the story.

We do not copy full articles or publish rewritten versions of publishers' reports. If a source image cannot be used, we show a neutral category placeholder instead of an unrelated picture.
