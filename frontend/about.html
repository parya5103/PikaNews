About PikaNews

PikaNews is a digital platform by GoGo Company, published at gogocompany.in/pikanews/.

What PikaNews Does

PikaNews is a digital news discovery platform. It automatically collects headlines, short excerpts and links from the public RSS feeds of established news publishers, organizes them into eight categories (Bollywood, Hollywood, Entertainment, Technology, Gaming, Business, Sports and Trending), and republishes them with clear source attribution every 30 minutes.

PikaNews is not the original publisher of the reports it shows. Every story names its original publisher and links to the full report on the publisher's own website. See News Sources & Disclaimer for the full list of sources and our Editorial Policy for our standards.

Why It Exists

Checking a dozen news sites takes time. PikaNews puts the latest verified headlines from trusted publishers in one fast, mobile-friendly place, so you can scan what happened and jump to the original report in one tap. It runs on a zero-cost, fully automated pipeline: public RSS feeds in, static pages out, no manual copying.

How It Works

  • A scheduled collector reads the public RSS feeds of all configured publishers every 30 minutes.
  • Stories are deduplicated, checked for missing or malformed fields, and categorized by topic.
  • Static pages, article pages, sitemaps and the RSS feed are regenerated from the collected data.
  • The GoGo Company central site publishes the result to gogocompany.in/pikanews/.
  • Stories that fail our quality checks are never promoted as top stories or placed next to advertising.
